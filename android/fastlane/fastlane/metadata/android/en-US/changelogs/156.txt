What's new in v1.9.49:
- Share an encryption key over NFC by holding two phones together; the app now turns NFC on for you if it is off.
- Clearer messages when a media upload does not finish.
- Updated Firebase, React, in-app billing, keyboard, networking, sound and QR code libraries for better reliability.
- Fixed several security advisories reported by Dependabot.
- Kept Babel 8 and TypeScript 7 on hold until the React Native toolchain supports them cleanly.
