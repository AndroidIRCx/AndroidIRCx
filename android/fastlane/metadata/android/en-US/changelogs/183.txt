What's new in v1.10.11:
- Replies, reactions and typing now work with other IRCv3 clients.
- Long messages are no longer cut off; long encrypted ones arrive whole.
- After a dropped connection you rejoin the channels you were in.
- Connection errors show the real reason. Tor and .onion get more time.
- Message history can no longer be wiped by a failed read.
- Firebase, camera, clipboard and picker library updates.
- Security hardening and smaller fixes.
